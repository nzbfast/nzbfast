  READ THIS AGAINST THE PER-RUNG TABLE AND NOT INSTEAD OF IT. The two
  answer different questions and both answers are real: no INDIVIDUAL
  flip is established (every one is reproduced by chance 21% to 82% of
  the time, and three of them sit in 8-member nulls whose smallest
  attainable P is 0.125), while the SET of them is not chance - which
  says steal predicts WHICH leg owns the A/A worst, and nothing about
  whether any one rung's verdict can now be read.

  AND THIS IS A CALIBRATION, NOT A SIZED HYPOTHESIS TEST. The statistic
  is computed on the round that raised the question, so 0.0032 prices
  the coincidence and does not license a decision - the same limit the
  guest-steal retrospective states about its own nulls.
