== ARM 6: is the curvature at 32-row spacing RESOLVED, or noise shaped like one?
   172 coarse ladder-metrics carry four or more consecutive 32-row rungs.
   Adjacent second differences DISAGREE in sign in 391 of 568 pairs = 69%.
   Pure per-rung noise predicts 73.2%; a resolved curvature predicts well under 50%.
   => the curvature a 32-row ladder appears to have is, to within a few per cent, its own rung scatter.
